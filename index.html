08 — For Salsa

A Little Letter

Sal,

Just imagine, out of everyone who has crossed paths in my life, somehow you're the one who stuck around the most. Even though I warned you that I'm a bit messy, always forget my charger, and get cranky whenever I'm hungry. Yet strangely enough, you're still right there, laughing at me every time I tell a corny joke.

Since November 29, 2025, so many little things have changed — the way I look at Mondays, how I save songs to my playlists, all the way to how I imagine the "future", which is now completely filled with you. I won't say you're perfect, because you get noisy when you're sleepy and always misremember movie titles, but that's exactly what makes everything feel like home.

Thank you for being by my side, for being patient, for putting up with me, and for always choosing to stay here even when there are plenty of reasons to be tired. Looking ahead, I only want one simple thing: to keep staying next to you, celebrating small moments like this, until we don't count the days anymore because we've been together for so long.

Happy early one year anniversary, Sal. I love you, so much — even more than your late-night mood swings at 11 PM.

— Fahrizal
